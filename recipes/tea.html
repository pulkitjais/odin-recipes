TEA

DESCRIPTION

Tea is an aromatic beverage prepared by infusion of hot or boiling fresh water with fresh or cured leaves of Camellia sinensis, an evergreen shrub native to East Asia that originated in the borderlands of southwestern China, northeast India and northern Myanmar.[3][4][5] Tea has a stimulant effect in humans primarily due to its caffeine content.

Ingredients

  • MILK
  • WATER
  • TEA LEAVES
  • SUGAR

Steps

  1. TAKE A CUP OF MILK AND ADD SOME WATER TO IT
  2. BOIL IT
  3. ADD TEA LEAVES TO IT
  4. ADD SUGAR ACCORDING TO TASTE
  5. COOK THIS ON LIGHT FLAME FOR 10 MINUTES
  6. YOUR TEA IS READY
